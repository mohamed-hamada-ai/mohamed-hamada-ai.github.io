Task type: image editing · user photo · rubric fulfillmentIllustrative example
Request
Make this photo black and white, but keep both of the cat's eyes in their original color. Add a soft vignette. Keep everything else the same.
Evaluator note

4 of 5 criteria met. The black-and-white conversion and the vignette are clean, and the photo is otherwise untouched. R3 fails: the request said “both” eyes, and only the left eye kept its color.

  1. R1Is the photo black and white, apart from the eyes?Yes. Fur, nose and background are all grey.YES
  2. R2Is the cat's left eye in its original color?Yes. The yellow-green iris matches the input.YES
  3. R3Is the cat's right eye in its original color?No. The right iris turned grey like the rest of the photo.NO
  4. R4Was a soft vignette added?Yes. The edges and corners darken gradually with no hard line.YES
  5. R5Is the rest of the original photo unchanged?Yes. Same framing, focus, whiskers and fur detail.YES
Criteria met 4 / 5Visual artifacts: minor, a faint warm fringe on the upper lid of the colored eye
Task type: LLM evaluation · side by side · 7-point scaleIllustrative example
User prompt
Explain to a 12-year-old why the sky is blue. Keep it under 80 words.
Response A67 words

Sunlight looks white, but it's really a mix of all the colors. When it enters our air, it bumps into tiny gas molecules. Blue light has short, quick waves, so it gets bounced around much more than red or yellow light. That scattered blue light comes at your eyes from every direction, so the whole sky looks blue. At sunset, light travels farther, and reds win instead.

Response B88 words

The sky is blue because it reflects the color of the oceans, which cover most of the Earth's surface. Water absorbs red light and gives off blue light, and the atmosphere acts like a giant mirror that bounces that blue color back down to us. This is also why the sky looks less blue over deserts. Scientists have studied this effect for hundreds of years, and it is one of the most famous facts in science. So next time you look up, remember you're really seeing the sea!

Source · 中文

请各部门于本周五下班前提交下月的生产计划，逾期将影响原材料的采购安排。

Raw MT

Please each department submit next month's production plan before Friday off work, overdue will affect raw materials purchase arrangement.

  1. E1“before Friday off work” · Accuracy下班前 means “by the end of the workday”, and 本周 (“this week”) was dropped.MAJOR
  2. E2“Please each department” · GrammarShould read “All departments are asked to…”.MINOR
  3. E3“overdue will affect” · Grammar逾期 needs a subject: “Late submissions will affect…”.MINOR
  4. E4“purchase arrangement” · StyleMore natural as “raw material purchasing schedule”.MINOR
Post-edited · EN

All departments are asked to submit next month's production plan by the end of the workday this Friday. Late submissions will affect the raw material purchasing schedule.
