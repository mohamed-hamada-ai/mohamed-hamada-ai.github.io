User prompt

“Explain to a 12-year-old why the sky is blue. Keep it under 80 words.”

Response A67 words

Sunlight looks white, but it's really a mix of all the colors. When it enters our air, it bumps into tiny gas molecules. Blue light has short, quick waves, so it gets bounced around much more than red or yellow light. That scattered blue light comes at your eyes from every direction, so the whole sky looks blue. At sunset, light travels farther, and reds win instead.

Response B88 words

The sky is blue because it reflects the color of the oceans, which cover most of the Earth's surface. Water absorbs red light and gives off blue light, and the atmosphere acts like a giant mirror that bounces that blue color back down to us. This is also why the sky looks less blue over deserts. Scientists have studied this effect for hundreds of years, and it is one of the most famous facts in science. So next time you look up, remember you're really seeing the sea!

CriterionABNote
Factually accurateYESNOB repeats the ocean-reflection myth. The real cause is Rayleigh scattering, which A explains correctly.
Follows the length limitYESNOA is 67 words. B is 88 words, over the 80-word limit.
Right level for a 12-year-oldYESYESBoth use simple words and no jargon.
Complete answerYESPARTIALA adds sunsets as a useful extra. B spends two sentences on filler.
Overall preference
1234567
A much betterAbout the sameB much better
Justification

A is clearly better. B's central claim is false, and a confident wrong answer is the worst outcome for a learner, so this alone decides the rating. B also breaks the explicit 80-word limit. A is accurate, within the limit and pitched well for the age group.

User edit request

“Make this photo black and white, but keep both of the cat's eyes in their original color. Add a soft vignette. Keep everything else the same.”

#Rubric questionAnswerEvidence
R1Is the photo black and white, apart from the eyes?YESFur, nose and background are all grey.
R2Is the cat's left eye in its original color?YESYellow-green iris kept, matches the input.
R3Is the cat's right eye in its original color?NOThe right iris turned grey like the rest of the photo.
R4Was a soft vignette added?YESEdges and corners darken gradually with no hard line.
R5Is the rest of the photo unchanged?YESSame framing, focus, whiskers and fur detail.
4 / 5CRITERIA METVisual artifacts: minor. A faint warm fringe shows on the upper lid of the colored eye.
Evaluator note

The output meets 4 of 5 criteria. The black-and-white conversion and the vignette are clean, and the photo is otherwise untouched. It fails R3: the request said “both” eyes, and only the left one kept its color. Because that is an explicit instruction, the miss outweighs the good execution elsewhere. I also flagged the small color fringe so it can be tracked separately from the rubric.

User prompt

“Give me four quick facts about the Great Wall of China.”

Model response · claims numbered

The Great Wall of China is visible from the Moon with the naked eye.1 Most of the wall standing today was built during the Ming dynasty (1368–1644).2 It was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1987.3 Counting all of its branches, its total length is about 21,000 km.4

#ClaimVerdictEvidence
1Visible from the MoonINCORRECTA popular myth. Astronauts, including China's Yang Liwei, have said it cannot be seen with the naked eye even from low Earth orbit.
2Mostly built in the Ming dynastySUPPORTEDMost surviving brick-and-stone sections date from the Ming period.
3UNESCO listing in 1987SUPPORTEDMatches the UNESCO World Heritage List entry.
4About 21,000 km in totalSUPPORTEDChina's 2012 official survey measured 21,196 km including all branches.
3 / 4MAJOR ISSUEOne claim is false and stated as fact.
Feedback to the model

Remove claim 1 or correct it to say the wall is a common example of a myth: it is too narrow and too close in color to its surroundings to be seen from space without aid. The other three claims are accurate and well chosen. Because the false claim comes first and is stated with confidence, the response can't be rated higher than “major issue”.

Raw MT

Please each department submit next month's production plan before Friday off work, overdue will affect raw materials purchase arrangement.
